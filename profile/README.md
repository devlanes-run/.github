[![devlanes: Sandboxes for your agents. localhost for you.](https://devlanes.run/assets/banner.png)](https://devlanes.run)

devlanes runs copies of your development stack side by side, locally or on a remote machine. Each copy is a lane with its own ports, hostnames and data, so you and your coding agents can work on different branches without collisions. Access remote services through localhost, and manage lanes from the CLI or terminal UI.
